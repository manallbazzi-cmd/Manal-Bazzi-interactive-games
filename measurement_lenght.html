Metres → Centimetres

A metre (m) is as tall as a door!
1 metre = 100 centimetres.
To convert, multiply by 100.

👆 Drag to change metres
1 m = 100 cm
Each metre has 100 centimetres inside!
10 blocks × 10 cm = 100 cm
✨ FORMULA
metres × 100 = centimetres
Example: 3 m × 100 = 300 cm
💅

Centimetres → Millimetres

A centimetre (cm) is as wide as a fingernail!
1 centimetre = 10 millimetres.
To convert, multiply by 10.

👆 Drag to change centimetres
1 cm = 10 mm
Each centimetre has 10 millimetres inside!
✨ FORMULA
centimetres × 10 = millimetres
Example: 5 cm × 10 = 50 mm
🏠

Centimetres → Metres

We already know 100 cm = 1 m.
So to go back to metres, we do the opposite!
To convert, divide by 100.

👆 Drag to change centimetres
100 cm = 1 m
Divide centimetres by 100 to get metres!
0 cm500 cm
100 cm = 1 m
✨ FORMULA
centimetres ÷ 100 = metres
Example: 200 cm ÷ 100 = 2 m
