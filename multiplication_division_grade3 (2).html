🌟 Key Points Summary
You explored the galaxy of multiplication and division!
✖️

Multiplication

Fast repeated addition. 3 × 4 = 12 means 3 groups of 4 (or 4+4+4).

➗

Division

Splitting equally into groups. 12 ÷ 3 = 4 means 12 shared among 3.

🔗

Fact Family

3×4=12, 4×3=12, 12÷3=4, 12÷4=3. Same 3 numbers — 4 facts!

📊

Arrays

Rows × Columns = Total. A rectangle of objects shows multiplication visually.

🔄

Commutative

Order doesn't matter! 3×4 = 4×3 = 12

🎯

Inverse Operations

Multiplication and division are opposites. If 6×7=42, then 42÷6=7 and 42÷7=6.

0️⃣

Zero Property

Any number × 0 = 0.
Any number × 1 = that number.

🔟

Multiply by 10

Just add a zero! 7 × 10 = 70, 9 × 10 = 90
