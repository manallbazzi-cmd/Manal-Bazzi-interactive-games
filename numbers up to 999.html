Key Takeaways

  • A 3-digit number has hundreds, tens, and ones places.
  • The hundreds digit tells how many groups of 100.
  • The tens digit tells how many groups of 10.
  • The ones digit tells how many single units.
  • Expanded form breaks a number apart: 647 = 600 + 40 + 7.
  • The greatest 3-digit number is 999 (9 hundreds + 9 tens + 9 ones).
