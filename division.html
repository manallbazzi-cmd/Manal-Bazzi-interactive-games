🏆 Key Takeaways — Division!

🔀
Division splits a total number into equal groups — just like sharing fairly!
🔢
Dividend ÷ Divisor = Quotient
Dividend = total · Divisor = groups · Quotient = each group's amount
🔗
Division is the opposite of multiplication! If 4 × 3 = 12, then 12 ÷ 4 = 3
♾️
Any number ÷ 1 = itself and any number ÷ itself = 1
🚫
You can NEVER divide by 0! It is impossible in mathematics.
