What is Length?

Length tells us how long or tall something is.

🐜 ✏️
Centimeters (cm)
We use cm for small things — like a bug, a pencil, or an eraser.
🚗 🌳
Meters (m)
We use m for BIG things — like a car, a tree, or your classroom.
