🔲 What is Perimeter?

Perimeter is the total distance around the outside of a shape. To find it, add up the length of ALL the sides!

P = Side 1 + Side 2 + Side 3 + Side 4

For a rectangle: P = 2 × length + 2 × width

🔢 Ordering Numbers

Numbers can be ordered from smallest to biggest (ascending): 3, 7, 12, 18, 25...

Or from biggest to smallest (descending): 25, 18, 12, 7, 3...

When we calculate perimeters, we can order them to compare which shape has the bigger or smaller perimeter!

Example: Perimeters of 8, 12, 16, 24 — which is biggest? → 24!
