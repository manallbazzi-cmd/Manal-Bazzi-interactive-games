🌟 What We Learned! 🌟

  • Subtraction means taking away! When we subtract, we remove things from a group.
  • The minus sign (-) means "take away". It tells us to make the number smaller.
  • Start with the big number. The first number tells us how many we have at the beginning.
  • Take away the second number. Remove that many items from the group.
  • Count what's left! The answer is how many remain after taking away.
  • Example: 5 - 2 = 3 means start with 5, take away 2, and you have 3 left!
