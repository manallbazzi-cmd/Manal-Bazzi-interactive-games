📖 Key Takeaways
🪙 Total: 0
🏛️

Place Value

Every digit has a place: Hundreds, Tens, Ones. The position changes its value!

5 in 534 = 500
5 in 253 = 50
5 in 375 = 5
📐

Expanded Form

Break a number into its place-value parts added together.

347 = 300 + 40 + 7
📝

Number Words

Say or write numbers using words. Hundreds + tens + ones combined.

629 =
"six hundred twenty-nine"
⚖️

Comparing

Compare hundreds first, then tens, then ones. Use < = >.

472 < 527
831 > 813
🔢

Ordering

Arrange numbers smallest to largest (ascending) or largest to smallest (descending).

241, 412, 421, 512
📏

Number Line

Numbers are evenly spaced on a line. Between any two numbers are more numbers!

0 ——— 500 ——— 999
