Key Things to Remember!

  • The digit on the right represents Ones (single pieces).
  • The digit on the left represents Tens (groups of 10).
  • The number 79 is made of 9 ones and 7 tens.
  • When ordering numbers, always look at the Tens first, and if they are the same, look at the Ones!
