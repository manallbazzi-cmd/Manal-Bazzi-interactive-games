🔑 Key Math Points to Remember:

  • 🧱
    Base-10 Blocks: A Hundreds Flat is 100 cubes, a Tens Rod is 10 cubes, and a Ones Unit is 1 cube.
  • 🔍
    Left to Right Rule: Always compare starting with the largest place value. Compare Hundreds first! If they match, compare Tens. If they also match, compare Ones.
  • 🐊
    The Hungry Gator (< and >): The alligator's mouth always opens towards the larger number! For example: $345 < 413$ because 400 is larger than 300!
  • ➕
    Expanded Form: Breaking up a number into hundreds, tens, and ones. $825 = 800 + 20 + 5$.
